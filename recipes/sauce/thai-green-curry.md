## Vegan Thai Green Curry (The Easy Way)

In this version, we'll use a store-bought curry paste from Thailand.

The upsides of this approach: It is super easy and fast. Also, if you don't have access to an Asian grocery store, this is probably your only option.

The downside of this approach: It may be too spicy for you. My wife and I can handle the spice, but most of our visitors can't.

### Vegan Curry Pastes

You can find these at an Asian grocery or on Amazon. The two curry pastes that are vegan are:
- Aroy-D: It is more spicy than the other option, but I also like the flavor more.
- Maesri: This is what we normally use. It's also available in smaller 4oz portions, and we use one 4oz can per person, which makes enough for dinner and leftovers. This route also helps measure out the coconut milk you'll need due to the instructions on the can.

Get one of those curry pastes. The Thai Kitchen green curry paste isn't anywhere near as good IMO, even though it is available at many grocery stores.
